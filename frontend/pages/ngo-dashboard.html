How Smart Matching Works

FoodBridge uses a rule-based matching algorithm to recommend the best food donations for your NGO. The match score considers:

📍 Distance — 40%

Closer donations score higher, ensuring practical pickup logistics.

🍽 Food Compatibility — 35%

Matching your food preferences, dietary needs, and quantity requirements.

⏰ Pickup Availability — 25%

Whether you can collect the food before the pickup deadline.

This is a rule-based algorithm, not a machine learning model. Scores are calculated from your profile data and donation details.
