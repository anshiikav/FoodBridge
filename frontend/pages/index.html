Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participating in FoodBridge

How does FoodBridge ensure food safety? +
Restaurants must complete a mandatory four-point food safety checklist confirming hygienic preparation, safe storage temperature, and clean packaging before listing. Automated pickup deadlines expire uncollected food safely.
How does the QR-based pickup confirmation work? +
When an NGO claims a donation, a single-use 6-digit confirmation code and encrypted QR code are generated. The restaurant kitchen displays the code, and the NGO verifies it on collection to update the status to Completed.
Is there any fee or subscription required? +
No. FoodBridge is completely free and non-commercial for verified charities, shelters, and food donors as a social impact initiative.

Our Approach to Food Safety

Every donation goes through a safety confirmation process

FoodBridge requires restaurants to complete a food safety checklist before listing any donation. This includes confirming preparation details, storage conditions, and packaging quality.

✓Preparation time must be recent and clearly stated
✓Pickup deadline is enforced — expired listings are auto-removed
✓Food safety checklist must be completed before publishing
✓Temperature and handling guidelines are shared with both parties
Important: FoodBridge facilitates connections between food donors and recipients. All food handling must comply with applicable local food safety regulations. This platform does not replace professional food safety inspections or certifications.
